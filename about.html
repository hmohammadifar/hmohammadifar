About me

Hossein Mohammadifar is a technology strategy, information security and digital transformation specialist and consultant with over 15 years of experience in banking and financial industries. His work spans IT governance, fintech architecture, project management and AI-based tools.

His experience includes leading large-scale initiatives, designing fintech platforms, establishing PMOs and aligning data-driven analytics and technology risk management with business strategy.
